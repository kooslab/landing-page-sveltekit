## Video 3 — Founder Story (~60 seconds)

> Hi, I'm Johnny. I run KooStory out of Berlin.
>
> I've spent the last several years building custom software for small businesses — backoffice tools, automation systems, internal apps. And what I kept seeing was the same thing over and over: companies would buy a tool, or hire developers, and nothing would actually change. Because the real problem wasn't technology. It was that nobody had written down how their business actually worked.
>
> A grocery store in Seoul was processing delivery orders by hand — sixty seconds each. We built a kiosk. Now it's ten seconds. A finance team was drowning in spreadsheets they were scared to touch. We automated the data flow. Problem gone.
>
> The tool isn't the hard part. Understanding the problem first — that's the hard part.
>
> That's what KooStory does. We start with your actual problem, not a product pitch. And if technology is the right answer, we build it or train your team to use it. If it's not — we'll tell you that too.
>
> If you have a process that's slow, manual, or breaking — book a free call. Thirty minutes. Let's figure out what's actually going on.

## Video 4 — What our workshop actually looks like (~90 seconds)

> I want to show you what actually happens in one of our workshops — because "hands-on AI training" means something very specific to us, and it probably looks different from what you're imagining.
>
> We don't do slide decks. We don't show you demos of ChatGPT doing impressive things. That's not useful.
>
> What we do is this: before the session, we ask you to bring one real problem from your work. Something you actually deal with. A report you write every week. A process that takes too long. An email you write twenty times a day.
>
> Then in the session — we solve it. Together. Live.
>
> Level 1 is about daily productivity. By the end of three hours, your team members are using AI for real tasks — their tasks, not made-up exercises. Level 2 goes into process design and automation. Level 3 — your non-technical employees actually build working web tools. Not prototypes. Real things.
>
> Every session is in person, in Berlin, small group. Because that's the only way it actually sticks.
>
> The benchmark I use: if you leave and nothing changes in your work next Monday — we failed. That's not what we're here for.
>
> If that sounds like the kind of training your team needs, book a free 20-minute call and tell me what problem you're trying to solve.
